# Brag Plan: StealthPay

## What is this app?
StealthPay is a Nimiq Pay mini app for contractors who get paid without putting a public wallet on the invoice. The payer sees name, service, and amount. Settlement is a normal NIM or USDT transfer. It is not a mixer.

## The angle
Privacy as an invoice, not as a conspiracy. Dark Nimiq-blue vault, gold CTA, honest limit on screen: **Private invoices. Not on-chain anonymity.** Show the generate → share → viewing-key flow, not a shielded-pool fantasy.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Gold hex on navy. STEALTHPAY. Kicker `[ VAULT // READ-ONLY KEYS ]`. Headline **Invoices without a public name.**

## Key moments (the middle)
- Generate card: Individual/Organization, **Northwind Studio**, **September retainer**, **500 NIM**
- Payer invoice: those three fields only. Line: **Wallet address stays off this page.**
- Viewing key: **Read-only ledger for accountants. No send.**

## Outro / punchline
**StealthPay.** Then the product’s own limit: **Private invoices. Not on-chain anonymity.**

## User flow worth showing
Entry: generate invoice (name, service, amount).
Key action: share QR/link; payer confirms in wallet.
Result: accountant viewing key; wallet never on the public page.
